I’m Bob. And I’m bothered.

Short pixel-art comedy about the small stuff that ruins everyone’s day. The speakerphone call in a quiet restaurant. The cart left in a parking spot. The seat that reclines into your knees. Bob notices all of it, so you don’t have to suffer alone.

The cast

  • Bob

    Tie always on, patience always off. He doesn’t want trouble. He just wants you to put the cart back.

  • Deadlift Dave

    Bob’s buddy. Benches 350, cries at commercials. Freshly divorced, back on the dating scene, and sure his blackjack system works this time.

  • Randy Ralph

    Top-selling real estate agent. 46, parties like he’s 25, and calls women his own age “vintage listings.” His face is on every bus bench in town. He also owns a pizza place, mostly by accident. Nobody remembers inviting him.

  • Sexy Sarah

    Divorce attorney who goes for blood. She got Darlene the house, which is why Dave lives in his truck. Her clients keep finding her. Nobody asks how.

  • Speakerphone Sandra

    Every call on speaker. Every FaceTime too.

  • Karen

    Would like to speak to the manager. Of the lemonade stand.

  • Gary

    Bob’s coworker. Eats your labeled yogurt, then replies all about it.

  • Sideline Steve

    Youth soccer dad. Has never played soccer.

  • Kenzie

    Always filming. The gym, the train, your concert view.

    Talks a huge game. Usually ends up on the ground.

  • Rick

Five shows, one channel

The main show. Bob runs into one everyday etiquette fail and loses his mind about it in under a minute.

The other side of the story. Sandra, Karen, Gary and the rest explain why they’re actually the victim.

After Hours 18+

Randy Ralph and Sexy Sarah, unsupervised. Bad decisions, worse pickup lines and a lot of bleeps. Not for the kids.
