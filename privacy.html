Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Bothered Bob (“we”, “us”) handles information for this website and for Bothered Bob Publisher, our internal publishing tool. Short version: we count visits to this website with Google Analytics, and we don’t sell or share anything about you.

1. This website

This website has no accounts and no forms. We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which links they follow (for example, to our TikTok or YouTube). It records things like pages viewed, clicks on outbound links, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and the site that referred you. It does not give us your name or contact details, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • Cookies. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell new visits from returning ones. If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, no analytics cookies are set; Google receives only cookieless, aggregated measurements.
  • No ads. Advertising features, ad personalization and Google signals are turned off, and we have turned off Google’s optional data-sharing settings.
  • Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. How Google handles this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Like any website, our hosting provider may also process basic technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages; we do not use that data.

2. Bothered Bob Publisher

Bothered Bob Publisher is used only by Bothered Bob to post its own videos to its own accounts. It is not available to the public.

  • Minimum permissions. It requests only the platform permissions needed to upload and publish our videos, for example TikTok video.upload and video.publish, YouTube upload, and Instagram content publishing.
  • Tokens. OAuth tokens granted by those platforms are stored securely on the company’s own machine. They are never sold or shared with anyone.
  • No user data. It collects no data from viewers or any other users. It does not read followers, comments, messages or viewer information.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Website analytics are processed by Google on our behalf as described above.

4. Deletion requests

If you believe we hold any information about you and want it deleted, or you have any privacy question, contact us and we will respond and delete any such information. You can also revoke Bothered Bob Publisher’s access at any time from the connected-apps settings of the platform account it was authorized on.

5. Contact

Send a direct message to Bothered Bob: @thebotheredbob on TikTok, @bothered.bob on Instagram, or @botheredbob on YouTube.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
